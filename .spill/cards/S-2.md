## Purpose

The single screen where the user dictates, reads and revises a piece. Simple, calm, and readable for long-form text. Build target: design variant B (manuscript with margin notes), with the review pins applied.

## Requirements

1. White paper background with a visible, grainy texture lit like an embossed card.
2. Top bar: the cursive C logo (the supplied SVG) on the left, the document breadcrumb (Library / title) in the centre, with no kind tag, and on the right a Proofread button, the auto-fixed count, a gray badge with the number of pending suggestions, and the version stepper.
3. One centered serif column for the document (about 680px measure).
4. Suggestions sit in the margins next to the lines they touch: wording and grammar notes on the left, each with Apply and Ignore.
5. Selecting or clicking highlighted text opens the Rephrase / Substitute / Sentence card on the right, and the span under review gets an animated (marching) outline until the card closes.
6. Clicking the pending badge opens a dropdown listing every pending issue (kind, short description, where it is). Clicking an item scrolls to it, highlights the span and opens its suggestion card, ready to apply or ignore.
7. Save (D-7, formerly Publish) is hidden while any suggestion is pending; it appears in the top bar once every suggestion is applied or ignored.
8. A single record button centered at the bottom, nothing else around it: idle it is a plain red circle; pressed, it turns into a stop button with a spinning ring, and the elapsed time shows under it.
9. Light theme in v1; the layout holds from 1100px to full screen width.

## Out of scope

- Dark theme.
- Custom fonts per document.

## Acceptance checks

- Built screen matches the accepted design side by side.
- Text stays readable (contrast >= 4.5:1) on the texture.
- Publish is absent with 1 pending suggestion and present with 0.
- Record button: idle shows a red circle, recording shows stop + spinning ring.
- The breadcrumb shows no Speech or Blog tag.
