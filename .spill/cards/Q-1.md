Cader needs a small, local, English-only text editing model for grammar fixes, rephrasing, word substitution and sentence alternatives. Research summary:

- **grammarly/coedit-large** (770M, FLAN-T5-large fine-tuned only on text editing instructions: fix grammar, paraphrase, make fluent, simplify, formalize). Exactly the "trained only for text editing" profile. ONNX exports exist (runs in onnxruntime-node or transformers.js). Weak at giving several distinct options and at single-word synonyms. License CC-BY-NC-4.0: fine for personal use, not for selling Cader.
- **Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (GGUF Q4, about 1 GB)** via node-llama-cpp with Metal. General small LLM, not text-only, but very good at "give 5 options, no repeats" with JSON output, and at precise vocabulary. Apache 2.0.
- **Small GEC fine-tunes** (e.g. T5-small/base grammar correction, about 60-250M; amiya/qwen2.5-3b-gec on MLX). Fast for proofreading, but only fix grammar, cannot rephrase.

Recommendation: C. CoEdIT-large does proofreading and the sentence-level fixes it was trained for; a small Qwen model does substitutions and multiple alternatives. Both run offline; about 2 GB on disk together.

- A. CoEdIT-large only (one text-editing model, smallest footprint, non-commercial license)
- B. Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct only (one general small LLM for every task, Apache 2.0)
- C. Both: CoEdIT-large for proofread/grammar, Qwen2.5-1.5B for rephrase, substitute and alternatives (recommended)
