## Purpose

Speak a speech or post into Cader and get accurate text into the document, fully on device, with no separate dictation app to install (D-3).

## Requirements

1. Cader runs the transcription engine Handy uses (whisper.cpp with Metal via the whisper-rs crate, plus Silero VAD) inside its Rust backend (D-3, D-6). The Handy app is not required.
2. The model is Whisper Large v3 Turbo (D-2, GGML, about 1.6 GB), downloaded on first use with a progress bar, cached in the app data folder and checksummed.
3. The record button in the dictation bar (and a global hotkey) starts and stops recording; the button states follow S-2 (red circle idle, stop with spinning ring while recording).
4. Microphone audio streams to the Rust backend; VAD trims silence; text is inserted at the cursor in the open document, or appended at the end when nothing is focused.
5. After stop, the bar shows a transcribing state until the text lands; a 60 second passage lands within 5 s on an M1.
6. Each dictation is kept as a raw transcript next to the document, so the original words can be recovered after revision.
7. Cader asks for microphone permission on first use and explains a denial with a link to System Settings.
8. Handy's MIT license notice is included for any code taken from it.

## Out of scope

- Speaker diarization, audio playback, audio storage beyond the session.
- Parakeet or other speech models in v1.

## Acceptance checks

- Dictating a 60 second passage inserts its text at the cursor within 5 s of stopping (M1).
- Stopping mid-sentence inserts the partial text.
- With the model not yet downloaded, pressing record starts the download instead of failing silently.
- Raw transcript file exists after a dictation.
- Works with the Handy app not installed.
