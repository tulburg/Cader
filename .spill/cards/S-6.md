## Purpose

Highlight part of a sentence and ask for a rephrase of that part, or a stronger, more precise word or phrase in its place.

## Requirements

1. Selecting text shows two actions next to the selection and in the right column: Rephrase and Substitute.
2. Rephrase returns 3 rewrites of the selected span that keep the meaning and fit the surrounding sentence.
3. Substitute returns up to 5 single words or short phrases that replace the span, favouring larger, more precise vocabulary, each with a short note on nuance.
4. Hovering an option previews it in place; clicking applies it; Esc restores the original.
5. "More" asks for another batch that does not repeat earlier options.
6. Keyboard shortcuts: Cmd+R rephrase, Cmd+E substitute, 1-5 to pick.

## Out of scope

- Tone presets (formal, casual) beyond what the model returns by default in v1.

## Acceptance checks

- Selecting "very big" and pressing Substitute offers words like "enormous" or "immense".
- Hover preview then Esc leaves the text unchanged.
- Three presses of More return no duplicate options.
