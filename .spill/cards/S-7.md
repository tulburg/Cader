## Purpose

For any sentence, see several different ways of saying exactly the same thing, and ask for more.

## Requirements

1. Placing the cursor in a sentence (or choosing Alternatives) lists 3 alternative versions of that sentence on the right.
2. Alternatives keep the meaning and differ in structure or emphasis, not only in single words.
3. Each alternative shows a diff against the original so the change is easy to see.
4. More asks for another batch without repeats; Pin keeps an alternative visible while trying others.
5. Choosing one replaces the sentence and records the change in the current review.

## Out of scope

- Paragraph-level rewrites.

## Acceptance checks

- A 15 word sentence shows 3 alternatives whose meaning matches (checked by hand on 10 sentences).
- More twice yields 9 distinct alternatives.
- Applied alternative appears in the version history diff.
