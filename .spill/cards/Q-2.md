Handy offers these Whisper models (GGML): Small 487 MB, Medium 492 MB (q4_1), Large v3 q5_0 1.1 GB, Large v3 Turbo 1.6 GB. You asked for a relatively large one. Turbo is close to Large v3 accuracy and several times faster, which matters for long speeches. Handy also offers Parakeet (English, CPU-optimized) if accuracy is equal for you.

- A. Large v3 Turbo (1.6 GB, recommended: large-model accuracy, fast)
- B. Large v3 q5_0 (1.1 GB, smaller download, slower)
- C. Let the user pick in Cader settings, default Turbo
