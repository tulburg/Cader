## Purpose

Check the whole piece for grammar, spelling and punctuation, and let the user accept or dismiss each fix.

## Requirements

1. The Proofread toolbar button checks the whole document, sentence by sentence, with progress shown.
2. Each issue is underlined in the text and listed as a card on the right with the original, the fix and a one-line reason.
3. Accept applies the fix; Ignore hides it for this version; Accept all applies every remaining fix.
4. Dictation artifacts (filler words like "um", repeated words, missing sentence punctuation) are flagged as their own kind.
5. Editing a sentence re-checks only that sentence.

6. Obvious misspellings and clearly out-of-place words are corrected automatically, without asking; the top bar shows how many were auto-fixed, and each auto-fix can be reviewed and undone.
7. Every other suggestion counts as pending until it is applied or ignored; the top bar shows the pending count, and Publish stays hidden while it is above zero.

## Out of scope

- Style guides (AP, Chicago).
- Plagiarism or fact checking.

## Acceptance checks

- A paragraph with five planted errors shows five issue cards.
- Accept all leaves no issue cards and the text matches the expected corrected text.
- Editing one sentence triggers one model call, not a full re-check.
