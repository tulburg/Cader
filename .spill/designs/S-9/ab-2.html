Tonight we are not just reopening a building. We are keeping a promise made to every child who ever sat cross-legged on that carpet and found a world inside a book.

The people who have worked on this renovation gave more than their time. They gave their weekends, their patience, and in a few cases, their actual blood, sweat and tears.
