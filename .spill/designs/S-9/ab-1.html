Remarks for the Riverside Library Opening

Good evening, and thank you all for coming. When this building first opened its doors in 1962, the town was smaller, the river was cleaner, and the idea of a public library was vital to the families who lived here.

Tonight we are not just reopening a building. We are keeping a promise made to every child who ever sat cross-legged on that carpet and found a world inside a book.

The people who have worked on this renovation gave more than their time. They gave their weekends, their patience, and in a few cases, their actual blood, sweat and tears when the old shelves came down.
